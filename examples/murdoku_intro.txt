# Murdoku 0 - "De eerste zaak"  (4x4, three regions)
#
# A first, tiny Murdoku to show how the puzzle works, made up for this repo
# in the style of a Murdoku book's opening example (which is not copied).
#
# Three suspects and one victim, one per row and column. Sanne, Bram and
# Femke each have a clue; Kees, the victim, takes the last free square. Kees
# was alone with his murderer: the one other person in his region did it.
#
# Solution: Sanne r1c2, Bram r2c4, Kees r3c3, Femke r4c1, so Femke is the
# murderer (she is the only other person in the slaapkamer).

Size: 4
Language: nl

Words:
bed: het bed
fornuis: het fornuis

Regions:
t: tuin #CBE6B9
k: keuken #FADFB5
s: slaapkamer #F4CBC3

Grid:
t t k k
t t k k
s s s k
s s s k

Objects:
boom: r1c1
fornuis: r1c4
kast: r3c1

# Chairs and the bed can be sat or lain on; the bed is 1x2.
Furniture:
stoel: r2c4 r4c4
bed: r4c1 r4c2

People:
Sanne
Bram
Femke
Kees

Clues:
next_to Sanne boom
on Bram stoel
on Femke bed
