# Murdoku 0 - "The first case"  (4x4, three regions)
#
# A first, tiny Murdoku to show how the puzzle works. The layout follows the
# opening example of a Murdoku book; people and rooms are renamed.
#
# Three suspects and one victim, one per row and column. Ingrid, Joost and
# Lotte each have a clue; Wouter, the victim, takes the last free square.
# Wouter was alone with his murderer: the one other person in his region
# did it.
#
# Solution: Joost r1c1, Wouter r2c3, Ingrid r3c4, Lotte r4c2, so Ingrid is
# the murderer (she is the only other person in the sitting room).

Size: 4
Language: en

Words:
patio: on the patio

Regions:
p: patio #CBE6B9
g: guest_room #F4CBC3
s: sitting_room #FADFB5

Grid:

p g s s
g g s s
g g s s

Objects:
tree: r1c2
bush: r1c3
table: r2c4
cabinet: r4c4

# Chairs and the bed can be sat or lain on; the bed is 1x2.
Furniture:

bed: r4c1 r4c2

People:

Wouter

Clues:
