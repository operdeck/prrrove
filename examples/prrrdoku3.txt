#
# Board based on the figure in proprietary puzzles/Prrrdoku 3.docx. For the late-reveal
# variant, r2c5 moves to Helmholtzstraat, and r1c6/r2c6 move to Klimgebied Noord
# so Bankastraat remains connected. Colours match the document's legend table.
# Cross-checked against the solution's candidate lists:
# vuurtje's neighbours outside the water are r4c1, r5c2; the
# banks are r1c2, r1c3, r3c4, r3c5, r8c3, r8c4; a knight's move from klimwand
# or boulder reaches r1c6, r3c6, r6c7, r6c9, r7c6, r9c6; eight free squares
# border a koffer in the koffer's own region; woonboot plus water has 19 free
# squares.

# The board first published put the koffer on r1c5 in Bankastraat. Since "next to"
# never crosses a region boundary, that left the puzzle without a solution; the
# document now has r1c5 in Helmholtzstraat, which keeps the published solution.
